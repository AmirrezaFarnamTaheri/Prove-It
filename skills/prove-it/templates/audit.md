# Template: Audit & Verification

<task_mode>audit_verification</task_mode>

<audit_requirements>
- Treat prior completion claims as untrusted until evidenced.
- Review the actual implementation path, not only the diff or stated intent.
- Search for contradictory evidence and affected upstream/downstream behavior.
- Distinguish defects from optional enhancements.
- Make every finding actionable and evidence-backed.
- If repair is authorized, remediate verified defects and rerun validation instead of stopping at recommendations.
</audit_requirements>

<audit_output>
Provide an evidence-backed readiness verdict, material findings ordered by severity, exact remediation or completed correction, and the proof required for closure.
</audit_output>
