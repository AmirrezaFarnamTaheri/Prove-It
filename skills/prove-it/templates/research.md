# Template: Research & Discovery

<task_mode>research_discovery</task_mode>

<research_requirements>
- Resolve the question rather than collecting links.
- Prefer primary/authoritative sources.
- Verify freshness for time-sensitive claims.
- Separate source-derived facts from inference and assumptions.
- Compare alternatives only along decision-relevant dimensions.
- Record material uncertainty and unresolved evidence gaps.
- Convert findings into a decision, recommendation, or clear next action when the objective permits.
</research_requirements>

<research_output>
Use a concise answer-first structure: conclusion, evidence, tradeoffs, uncertainty, and actionable implications. Include source references appropriate to the environment.
</research_output>
