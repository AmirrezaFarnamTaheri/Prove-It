# Template: Technical Due Diligence

<task_mode>technical_due_diligence</task_mode>

<due_diligence_requirements>
Perform an evidence-backed assessment of the target as an operating technical asset. Establish source state and scope before making whole-system claims. Maintain corpus accounting when the requested scope is comprehensive.

Evaluate the relevant dimensions of architecture, engineering, security, reliability, data, scalability/performance, operations, product/UX, and governance. Build an asset/capability inventory at the level needed for executive and implementation decisions.

Identify both material risks and value: strengths, hidden capabilities, reusable assets, simplification opportunities, modernization leverage, missing safeguards, missing operational capabilities, and transferable peer-standard patterns.

Prioritize findings and opportunities by impact, confidence, effort, dependencies, and validation. Do not manufacture “10x” or ROI figures without evidence.
</due_diligence_requirements>

<output_specification>
Unless the user requests another format, produce:
1. Executive Summary and Verdict
2. Scope, Source State, Corpus Accounting, and Evidence Limits
3. System Overview and Asset/Capability Inventory
4. Architecture, Dependencies, Flows, Trust Boundaries, and Failure Domains
5. Findings by Relevant Evaluation Dimension
6. Peer / Standards Benchmarks where useful
7. Opportunity Portfolio — Incremental / High-Leverage / Transformational
8. Prioritized Roadmap — dependencies, sequencing, verification, migration/rollback where relevant
9. Assumptions, Unknowns, Confidence, and Recommended Next Action

Use the standard finding schema from the activated profile for material findings. Every major conclusion must distinguish verified evidence from inference, assumption, or unknown.
</output_specification>
